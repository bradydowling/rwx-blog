SlimeBA Jam

MATCH OPTIONS

Rotate to landscape for larger controls. BALL picks up; hold and release it to throw. Add this page to your home screen from your browser menu.

Before tipoff: W/S or ↑/↓ choose a row · A/D or ←/→ change its value · Enter start. Both players share the cursor.

Play mode: Highlight Player 1 or 2, then press F, ., or Space (or click HUMAN/CPU) to toggle. Only one player can be CPU.

Player 1: A/D move · W or Space jump · S pick up, then hold/release to throw · F shove

Player 2: ←/→ move · ↑ jump · ↓ pick up, then hold/release to throw · . shove

First to 5 · R choose teams again after a win
